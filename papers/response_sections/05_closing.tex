\section*{Closing}

We again thank the Area Editor, the Associate Editor, and the referees for their careful and constructive guidance. Their comments led us to make a substantial revision rather than a local edit: the manuscript now frames the problem around exogenous arrivals, endogenous KV-cache memory growth, eviction-induced restarts, and latency; states the modeling scope and memory mechanism more explicitly; presents WAIT and Nested WAIT as fluid-guided scheduling policies that reduce the memory-coupled state space to threshold and boundary queues; and evaluates the policies on both synthetic and real-data workloads, using clearer visualizations and adding long-horizon checks and supplemental real-GPU validation. We hope the revised manuscript now conveys both the contribution and the scope of the results more clearly.

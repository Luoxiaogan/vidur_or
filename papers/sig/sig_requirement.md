Call for Papers:

Service Management

2026 SIG Day

HBS, BU, and MIT
 July 12, 2026

The Service Management Special Interest Group (SIG) of the Manufacturing and Service Operations Management (MSOM) Society will hold its annual conference ("Service Management SIG Day") on July 12, 2026, preceding the MSOM 2026 conference (July, 13-14). 

On behalf of the Service Management SIG, we are pleased to invite you to submit papers to this conference. Acceptance decisions will be based on full-paper submissions. In-person presentations of the accepted papers will be scheduled in a single track. We will focus on curating high-quality contributions that ensure a cohesive and diverse program. We encourage submissions of applied, empirical, methodological, or theoretical papers that delve into any area within the expansive and evolving field of service operations.


Important Dates 

Manuscript Central Submission Opens: January 9, 2026

Submission Deadline: January 30, 2026

Please note that no extensions will be given for the submission deadline. To facilitate the review process, we encourage you to submit your papers as early as possible. Notification of acceptance: May 8, 2026


Submission Guidelines and Review Process

Submission portal: Please submit your paper via the following manuscript central submission site (please note that this is different from the MSOM Journal submission site): 

https://mc.manuscriptcentral.com/msomconference


SIG and MSOM main conference. SIG authors who want their papers to be considered for the MSOM main conference presentation (in case of rejection by the SIG) should also submit an extended abstract to the MSOM main conference separately. Please indicate that you submitted your paper to SIG when submitting to the MSOM main conference. In this case, the SIG and the MSOM Conference will conduct independent reviews. The SIG-Day Event Co-Chairs will communicate their lists of accepted papers to the MSOM Conference Chairs so that accepted SIG-Day Event presentations may be removed from consideration for an MSOM Conference presentation slot.

Formatting requirements. Each paper should comply with MSOM journal's formatting standards: https://pubsonline.informs.org/page/MSOM/submission-guidelines.

Number of submissions. The same work can be presented only once during the entire conference period. Therefore, please do not submit the same work to multiple SIGs. Also, each attendee is limited to one submission for the MSOM conference, one submission for each SIG Meeting, and two presentations during the entire conference period.

Further information. Additional guidelines may be announced by each SIG through INFORMS CONNECT.


MSOM Journal and SIG Dual Purpose Submission

Authors can choose from two distinct submission options for SIGs, each following a separate review and decision process. The first option is the traditional SIG review option while the second option allows a dual submission to MSOM journal.

●      Option 1: Single Purpose-SIG-Day presentation

○      Review Process: Handled by SIG-Day Event Co-chairs

○      Decision makers: SIG-Day Event Co-chairs

●      Option 2: Dual Purposes-(1) SIG-Day presentation, (2) 1st round review (1RR) by MSOM Journal

○      Eligibility: Papers not under review at any other journals.

○    Review Process: Handled by SIG-Day Event Co-chairs (for SIG day) and by MSOM Journal review team in MSOM format (for MSOM journal).

○      Decision makers:

■      SIG-Day Co-chairs make SIG Day Presentation Decisions

■      MSOM DEs make MSOM Journal Decisions

○      Decision:

■      SIG Day Presentation and MSOM Journal Decisions are independent.

■      Papers recommended for Major Revision at MSOM continue at MSOM with the same review team.

■      Papers not recommended for Major Revision at MSOM may be submitted to MSOM as new submissions.

## MSOM Journal Formatting Standards (SIG Full Paper须遵守)

来源: https://pubsonline.informs.org/page/MSOM/submission-guidelines

### Page Limit

- 正文不超过 **32 pages**，包括 references, tables, figures, appendices（使用 MSOM LaTeX 或 Word 模板排版时的页数）
- 可另附 **Online Supplement**（单独文件），不超过 **16 pages**

### 排版格式

- **Double spacing**（每页不超过 33 行）
- **1-inch margins**（四边）
- **11-point font**
- **Single column**
- 不使用 footnotes
- 提交格式：**PDF**

### 匿名审稿 (Double-Blind)

- 正文中不含作者姓名、致谢、含作者名的 running headers
- 需准备两个版本：(a) 完整版含 title page；(b) 去除作者信息的匿名版

### LaTeX 模板

- INFORMS 官方 LaTeX 模板：https://pubsonline.informs.org/authorportal/latex-style-files
- Overleaf 模板：https://www.overleaf.com/latex/templates/tagged/informs
- 使用 `informs3` document class，选项为 `msom`
- 模板包含：journal template, style file, BibTeX style, PDF documentation

### SIG Day 提交特别说明（2026 MSOM Conference）

来源: https://www.hbs.edu/about/research/msom-conference-2026/submissions

- SIG Day 提交 **full paper**（非 extended abstract）
- 截止时间：**January 31, 2026, 11:59pm ET**（注意：SIG Call for Papers 写的是 January 30，Conference 官网写的是 January 31，以较早者为准）
- 提交 portal：https://mc.manuscriptcentral.com/msomconference
- 被拒的 SIG 论文可能被考虑在 July 14 进行 **poster presentation**
- Submitting/corresponding author 必须是现场 presenter

### MSOM Main Conference Extended Abstract（如需同时提交）

- 不超过 **3 pages**
- 1-inch margins, 11-point font, 每页最多 33 行
- 不含作者姓名（匿名审稿）
- 包含：title, research problem, methodology, results summary
- References 包含在 3 页以内
- 截止时间：**February 27, 2026, 11:59pm ET**

### 提交限制汇总

| 类别 | 限制 |
|------|------|
| Main conference 提交 | 每人最多 1 篇 |
| SIG 提交 | 每人每个 SIG 最多 1 篇 |
| 同一工作 | 不可同时提交多个 SIG |
| 整个会议期间 presentations | 最多 1 次（SIG 或 main，不能重复） |
| Dual submission (Option 2) | 论文不能在其他期刊 under review |
